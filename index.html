HOW TO PLAY

A true bartender doesn't merely memorize recipes. A bartender perceives relationships.

1
Group 16 Cards into 4 Sets of 4

Find four hidden connections spanning cocktail archetypes, stemware, ice chemistry, historical formulas, and legal specs.

2
Select 4 & Tap "Submit Pour"

Watch out for intentional overlap: four cards might feature rum, but only three are authentic Sours.

3
Mind the 4-Mistake Limit

Keep your station neat. If 3 of your 4 selections are correct, you will receive a "One Away" hint.
